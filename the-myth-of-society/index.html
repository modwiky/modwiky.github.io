The Myth Of Society

Society is a concept. It doesn't exist. It is nothing but big network of individual's desire and fear. It is the land conflicting interest. It is the world.

They want you to do it their way. They want you to agree. Rules and punishment.

It's said that if you want peace you must yield to the norm. and obey the rules. and be nice!

that's might be true if you want what they want. if you are one of the bottle. but remember, nature doesn't make a copy. yes, we are all a human being after all but each of us are unique. and the idea of one-fits-all is total bonkers.

if only we can overcome the fear of not belonging. and stand in our own feet. far from any road. away from the things of man.
